# RetailPulse Business Intelligence Project | IBM Cognos Analytics

## About the Project

This project showcases a collection of six practical exercises performed using **IBM Cognos Analytics** as part of my BCA in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence.

The project focuses on exploring retail sales and customer data through interactive reports, data filters, crosstabs, prompts, and dashboards. It demonstrates how business intelligence tools can help organize data, summarize key metrics, and present information in a format that supports business decision-making.

## Project Highlights

The project covers the following areas of business intelligence and reporting:

- **Sales Reporting:** Creating list reports to display regional sales, product categories, quantities, and revenue.
- **Data Grouping:** Organizing sales records by region and store, with aggregated totals.
- **Data Filtering:** Applying region-based and date-range filters to analyze specific subsets of data.
- **Crosstab Analysis:** Comparing sales and quantities across regions and product categories.
- **Interactive Reporting:** Building prompt pages with region, date-range, and cascading store selections.
- **Customer Analytics:** Designing a dashboard to visualize customer activity, new versus returning customers, and loyalty segments.

## Practical Work Completed

| Practical | Description |
